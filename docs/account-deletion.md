# FlyConnect — Account & Data Deletion

**Last updated:** 20 June 2026
**App:** FlyConnect (`com.urbansyncinnovations.flyconnect`)
**Developer:** Urban Sync Innovations

This page explains how to delete your FlyConnect account and the data associated with it, what is removed, and what (if anything) is retained. It applies to all FlyConnect users.

---

## How to delete your account

You can delete your account in two ways.

### Option 1 — In the app (recommended, immediate)

1. Open **FlyConnect** and sign in.
2. Go to **Settings** (Profile → gear icon).
3. Scroll to **Account** and tap **Delete Account**.
4. Read the confirmation, then confirm.
5. For your security you may be asked to **sign in again** before deletion completes.

Your account and the data listed below are removed immediately when the deletion finishes.

### Option 2 — By email request

If you can't access the app (for example, you've lost your sign‑in), email us and we will delete your account and data on your behalf:

- **Email:** privacy@flyconnect.co
- **Subject:** "Account deletion request"
- **Include:** the email address (or phone number) associated with your FlyConnect account, so we can verify ownership.

We respond to verified requests within **30 days**.

---

## What data is deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

- Your **user profile** (name, email, phone number, airline, position, bio, profile photo).
- Your **saved posts**, **blocked‑user list**, and **followers / following** lists.
- Your **trip / passport history**.
- Your **Firebase Authentication** record (your login identity).
- Your **push‑notification (FCM) token** and device identifiers tied to your account.
- Your **uploaded photos** associated with the deleted content above.

## What is retained, and why

To keep the community readable and to meet legal obligations, a limited set of data is **not** fully deleted:

- **Posts you authored** are **anonymized**, not removed. The author is shown as **"[deleted user]"**, your name and photo are detached, and the post is flagged as deleted. This preserves conversation context (replies and likes from other users) without identifying you. If you want specific posts removed entirely before deleting your account, delete those posts individually first.
- **Messages you sent** in one‑to‑one or group chats may remain visible to the other participants, detached from your profile. Delete sensitive messages before deleting your account if you need them gone.
- **Encrypted backups** may contain your data for up to **30 days** after deletion, after which they are permanently purged.
- **Limited records** may be retained longer only where required to comply with law, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, or enforce our Terms of Service.

We do **not** retain your data for advertising or sell it to third parties.

## Deletion timeline

| When | What happens |
|------|--------------|
| Immediately (in‑app) or within 30 days (email request) | Profile, lists, trips, auth identity, and push token deleted; authored posts anonymized |
| Within 30 days | Data purged from encrypted backups |
| Longer (rare) | Only data we are legally required to keep |
